Balloon Breeze: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026. Contact: altinasupport@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your progress (scores, crystals, unlocks, settings) is stored on your device (and, only if you sign in to Google Play Games, in your Play Games cloud save). We do not have accounts and we do not collect your name, email or location.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded video ads are optional: you choose to watch one for an extra life, bonus crystals, a second daily gift or a card pack. A short full-screen ad may also appear between runs (never for players who have made any purchase in the game). In the EEA and UK we ask for consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice in Settings; without consent ads are not personalised. AdMob may process device identifiers as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Anonymous play statistics

With your consent where it is required, the game may send anonymous play events (for example that a run started or ended, a boss was beaten, or a pack was opened) to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see where players get stuck. These events carry no name, email or location.

Google Play Games

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your score, achievements and saved progress are stored by Google under your Play Games profile. The game works fully without signing in.

Purchases

Optional one-time purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive no payment details.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions: altinasupport@gmail.com
